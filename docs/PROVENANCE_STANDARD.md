# Provenance and evidence standard

Each record has a unique neutral ID such as EVT-001 or EXH-001.

## Confidence categories

- **Primary record available:** original notice, email, voucher, recording, receipt, or screenshot inspected.
- **Contemporaneous account:** observation documented near the time of the event.
- **Retrospective account:** firsthand recollection recorded later.
- **Draft correspondence:** a letter or complaint that was prepared; does *not* prove submission.
- **Secondary summary:** a later compilation of reported events.
- **Unverified / unresolved:** a claim or question still requiring an original record or agency response.

## Fields to maintain

Event date; documentation date; source ID; author/custodian; original format; SHA-256 (original); redacted counterpart; facts directly visible in source; reported recollections; interpretations (separate); outstanding corroboration; disposition; verified delivery / receipt, if any.

Never convert an allegation, recollection, or unanswered question into a finding. Preserve competing explanations where warranted. Refer to applicable laws only after current legal verification.
